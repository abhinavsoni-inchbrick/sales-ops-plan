New ideas

Each new idea includes the question, insight, manager action, and a meeting decision state.

1.9 Monthly Dial → Connect → Prospect Funnel

Cadence: Monthly · Complexity: Low

Question: How many dials turned into connects and prospects each month?

Insight: Three-bar grouped monthly visualization highlights volume vs quality.

Manager action: Investigate flat connects with dropping prospects or rising prospect rates from better lists.

1.11 Golden Hour Heatmap

Cadence: Weekly · Complexity: Low

Question: Which hour and weekday have the best connect rate?

Insight: Hour × weekday colour grid surfaces the highest-performing call windows.

Manager action: Reserve peak dialing hours and block admin tasks then.

1.12 Number List Decay Chart

Cadence: Weekly · Complexity: Low

Question: When does a lead batch stop working?

Insight: Weekly junk% and unanswered% by batch reveal stale lists.

Manager action: Retire batches crossing 40% junk by week three.

1.13 Speed-to-First-Dial Report

Cadence: Daily · Complexity: Medium

Question: How quickly does each agent dial a new lead?

Insight: First dial within 5 minutes converts much better.

Manager action: Flag leads undialled for 30+ minutes and set a 15-minute standard.

1.14 First Call vs Repeat Attempt Connect Rate

Cadence: Monthly · Complexity: Low

Question: Do we connect better on the first call or later attempts?

Insight: Connect rates by attempt identify when to stop dialing.

Manager action: Limit retries and move stale numbers to alternative channels.

1.15 Live Daily Target Progress

Cadence: Daily · Complexity: Medium

Question: Are agents on pace to hit today’s dial targets?

Insight: Real-time progress bars show projected end-of-day performance.

Manager action: Intervene early for agents below 40% by midday.

1.16 Day-of-Week Performance Trend

Cadence: Monthly · Complexity: Low

Question: Are some weekdays consistently weaker?

1.17 Redial Gap Tracker
